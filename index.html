The Furuta Pendulum Visualization Lab

Ever wonder how to connect the ideas between dynamics and control? MayThe Furuta pendulum is a classic benchmark in control theory, featuring a rotary arm driven by a single motor that balances an inverted pendulum above it.

A rotary arm driven by a single motor balances an inverted pendulum above it — one input, two angles, a beautiful control problem. This lab walks the full arc: derive the equations of motion, design an LQR balance controller with an energy-based swing-up, then fly the whole thing in an interactive 3D simulator.
