The main ideas

What the derivation establishes

1  Equations of motion

Lagrange's equations on the two generalised coordinates give a coupled system \mb{M}\ddot{\vb{q}} + \mb{C}\dot{\vb{q}} = \vb{\uptau}, with the mass matrix depending on the pendulum angle because the arm must share angular momentum:

\mb{M} = \begin{bmatrix} J{}_\theta + B\sin^2\varphi & -L\cos\varphi \\ -L\cos\varphi & J{}_\varphi \end{bmatrix}
J{}_\theta = m{}_A l{}_B^2 + \tfrac{1}{4} m{}_B l{}_B^2 + {}^B J{}_3, \quad J{}_\varphi = \tfrac{1}{4} m{}_A l{}_A^2 + {}^A J{}_1, \quad L = \tfrac{1}{2} m{}_A l{}_A l{}_B

Only bar A carries potential energy, V = m{}_A g \tfrac{l{}_A}{2}\cos\varphi, and the actuator moment is the sole non-conservative term.

2  Linearisation about upright

At \vb{q} = \dot{\vb{q}} = \vb{0} the Christoffel matrix vanishes and the model becomes \dot{\vb{x}} = \mb{A}\vb{x} + \mb{B}M(t) with \vb{x} = [\varphi,\ \theta,\ \dot{\varphi},\ \dot{\theta}]^T. Gravity enters through \varphi alone, \theta is a pure integrator, and the pair is controllable (rank 4) — which is what makes an LQR design admissible at all.

3  Swing-up and balance

With one input and two coordinates, no linear law spans the whole state space. The pendulum energy E = \tfrac{1}{2}J{}_\varphi\dot{\varphi}^2 + m{}_A g\tfrac{l{}_A}{2}(\cos\varphi - 1) is pumped by saturating the torque against \operatorname{sgn}(\dot{\varphi}\cos\varphi) until the pendulum is near the top, where a state-feedback law takes over. The two are joined by a finite-state machine with hysteresis.
